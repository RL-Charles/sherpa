# Code of Conduct

Sherpa follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and make this a welcoming place
for people of every background and experience level. Harassment, insults and personal attacks
are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is
represented.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through
GitHub ([@RL-Charles](https://github.com/RL-Charles)). Reports are handled confidentially, and the
maintainer may remove comments, close threads or block participants who break this code.
