## Description

Legal and community scaffolding for the public repo:

- `LICENSE`: MIT, © 2026 RL-Charles and Sherpa contributors
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` (Contributor Covenant 2.1), `SECURITY.md` (GitHub private vulnerability reporting, enabled on the repo), `CHANGELOG.md`
- Issue and PR templates under `.github/`
- `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`: all dependencies are MIT, BSD-3-Clause or Apache-2.0
- README: badges, binary install, Contributing and License sections

## Why

Without a license nobody can legally use, fork or contribute to the code, and without community files there's no stated way to report bugs or vulnerabilities. Part of S-015 (Open-source readiness), alongside the scrub (T-0056) and CI/release (T-0058).

**Open for review:** confirm MIT is the license you want and that the copyright holder line is right.
