## Description

Make a ticket scannable in seconds instead of reading its whole history.

- **Worklog highlights:** routine progress entries fold behind a count (`w` expands them). Decisions, verifies, blockers and submits stay visible.
- **Review card:** items in `review` / `agent_review` show a card under the header with the submit message, how it was verified, follow-ups, the check count and warnings (no PR, no Now line).
- **PR marker:** tickets with a linked PR get a ⇡ before the title in the tree, coloured by PR state.

Code: `internal/worklog/worklog.go`, `internal/tui/tree.go`.

## Why

As agents logged more, reviewers had to scroll through every progress line to find what mattered for a verdict. Part of S-014 (Clear tickets): the review card is what the Needs-you inbox (T-0064) and `shp show` now lead with.

**Open for review:** no automated tests cover folding or the ⇡ marker, so check them by eye in the TUI.
