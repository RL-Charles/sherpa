## Why

The sherpa-app desktop viewer (a separate Qt app) needs the same view of a
project as the TUI, and must change work exactly as the TUI does, without
parsing or writing `.sherpa/` itself.

## Context

- Consumer: sherpa-app (C++/Qt Quick), which runs `shp app …` in the project
- The TUI's status picker, inbox approve/send-back and body save held their
  own copies of the logic

## Design

- `shp app snapshot` / `shp app item <id>`: JSON feed with rollups,
  staleness, reconciled Where, signals, review card and worklog sessions.
- `internal/actions`: SetStatus, Approve, SendBack, SaveBody, used by the
  TUI and by `shp app status|approve|reject|edit`, so both apply the same
  workflow rules and journal the same events.

## Out of scope

- The desktop app itself (its own repository)
