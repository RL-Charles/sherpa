## Description

Agents can run `shp done <id> --approved-by <human>` when a human has explicitly told them to close the work. The journal keeps the real actor (the agent) and records who approved it.

Without `--approved-by`, an agent's `done` is refused. Tickets that are not yet in review are refused too.

## Why

Closing tickets is a human decision, but "close T-0041 and T-0048" in chat shouldn't mean the human has to go and click each one. This lets the agent act on that instruction while the audit trail still shows who decided. It's rule 7 of the agent work protocol (S-002).
