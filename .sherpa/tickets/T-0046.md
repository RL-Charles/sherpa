## Why

Tickets are units of execution: once the deliverables exist and the checks prove them, an agent should be able to close the ticket without a human click. Stories and epics are units of outcome: their closure is the human review/approval step after the work beneath them is done. This keeps the human gate where judgement matters and stops it from becoming ceremony on every ticket.

## Context

- **Background:** the previous rule required a named human for every agent close. That made humans a bottleneck for tickets whose evidence was already complete.
- **Constraints:** closure must be evidence-based. A ticket needs at least one deliverable and one check; open manual checks still wait for a human. `--approved-by` remains available as optional audit for tickets, but it is not required.
- **Related:** T-0054 (agent review), T-0062 (independent reviewer), T-0064 (needs-you inbox). The inbox remains the human path for stories/epics and reviews.
- **Policy:** `auto_done` now requires a ticket, completed deliverables and resolved checks; it never auto-closes a story or epic.

## Design

### Shape

Closure is kind-aware. `CheckTransition` keeps the ordinary status rules and still blocks an agent from setting `done` directly. `CheckDone` is the closure policy used by `shp done`:

- human: ordinary transition rules;
- agent + ticket: submitted, at least one deliverable all done, at least one check all resolved;
- agent + story/epic: refuse.

`--approved-by` is recorded if provided but does not unlock a story or epic.

### Types and enums

~~~go
type ActorKind string
const (
    Human ActorKind = "human"
    Agent ActorKind = "agent"
)

type Actor struct {
    Kind ActorKind
    Name string
}

// model.Kind: Ticket | Story | Epic
// model.Status: Review | AgentReview | Done
// journal.Event.ApprovedBy: optional human on a ticket close
~~~

### Pattern

Policy gate at the transition boundary, keyed by item kind and evidence. The ordinary transition and the closure policy are separate functions, so `shp status` cannot bypass the ticket evidence rules and no command accidentally closes a story or epic.

### Invariants

- An agent cannot set `done` through `shp status` or the TUI status picker.
- An agent may close a ticket only from `review` or `agent_review`.
- A ticket has at least one deliverable and at least one check before an agent can close it.
- Every deliverable must be marked done; every check must be resolved (done or waived).
- Open manual checks block agent closure until a human ticks them.
- An agent can never close a story or epic, even with `--approved-by`.
- A human can close any kind through the ordinary transition rules.
- The journal keeps the actor; `approved_by` is optional audit for tickets.

### Contracts

- **Input:** `shp done <id>`, optional `--approved-by <human>` and `-m`.
- **Ticket success:** status becomes `done`; the journal records actor, from, to=done, optional approver and commit.
- **Ticket refusals:** not submitted; no deliverables; no checks; an open deliverable; an unresolved check; a manual check.
- **Story/epic:** an agent close is always refused; a human close is allowed after review.
- **Policy:** `auto_done` requires a ticket, completed deliverables and resolved checks.

### Edge cases

- A ticket with waived checks is closeable; waived counts as resolved.
- A ticket with a done deliverable but an open check is refused.
- A ticket with all checks resolved but a deliverable not marked done is refused.
- A ticket in `in_progress`, `ready`, `backlog` or `blocked` is refused until submitted.
- An agent may close from `agent_review` without waiting for the review to pass.
- `--approved-by` on a story or epic does not override the human-only rule.
- A human can close an old ticket with no deliverables or checks; the evidence rule applies to agents.
- Open checks are not a hard gate for a human close; humans can still close, but the review card shows them.

### Alternatives

- Keep human approval for every ticket: safe, but turns evidence-complete tickets into a queue.
- Auto-close on submit: removes the explicit "all deliverables and checks" check and surprises authors.
- Auto-roll-up stories and epics from child tickets: tempting, but closure is a human judgement about the outcome, not just a count.

## Out of scope

- Automatic story/epic roll-up when all child tickets are done.
- Changing the review inbox UI for stories and epics.
- Verified identity for an optional `--approved-by` audit name.
- Changing who may reopen a done item.

## Risks

- Agents can close weak tickets if the deliverables and checks are weak; `shp lint` and review are the quality controls.
- `agent_review` can be bypassed by a criteria-complete ticket; review is optional for tickets by design.
- Policy `auto_done` and manual closure must stay aligned on deliverables and checks.
