## Why

Long-running repos (433 tickets, tickets with 500+ journal events) buried the
current state under prose and history, and agents resuming a ticket got no
Now, outcome, handoff or recent decisions.

## Context

- Proposal: docs/ticket-clarity-proposal.md (phases 0, 1 and 3)
- Measured on a large real project: before/after TUI and session-start captures

## Design

- TUI: 4-line pinned strip (breadcrumb, staleness, capped Now, Next/Blocker),
  overview-first preview (signals → deliverables → reconciled Where → prose),
  `B` brief, `[`/`]` sections, `H` hide done, rollups, recency-sorted board.
- Agents: `shp handoff`, a resume card at session start, recency-ranked
  queues with stale work collapsed, Where reconciled against touched files,
  native session/branch/worktree on journal events and live pulses, OpenCode
  plugin cache invalidation, dead active-checkout cleanup.

## Out of scope

- MCP server (S-007)
